How this is ranked

Score is a weighted mean of OpenCode's six normalized 0–100 aspects: Reasoning 15%, Coding 30%, Cost efficiency 10%, Context window 10%, Multimodal 5% and Tool use 30%. Hover or focus a Score to see the per-aspect values and weights; the aspect heatmap shows them for the top 12 models.

Value balances Score with the estimated 5-hour request allowance. Score/$ divides Score by the token-mix blended price per 1M tokens; free models show Free. Top picks exclude limited-time free models from overall and value, but allow them for quality. A model is New for 14 days after release and Limited-time when OpenCode lists no monthly usage limit.

Retention vs Score is a reality check: retention is the share of users who return the next week. Trusted leaders score well and keep users; "hype" scores well but does not. Only models with retention data are plotted.
